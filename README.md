# Royal300 Growth Engine

Create a single-page, premium digital marketing agency portfolio website for ROYAL300.

Design direction: futuristic, minimal, premium, whitish/bright, glassmorphic, slightly 3D, editorial, technology-inspired, high-conversion, award-winning-agency style. Combine Apple-level minimalism + modern SaaS aesthetics + premium creative agency design + subtle 3D technology visuals. Not overly colorful. Color palette: primary pure white / off-white / very light gray / soft silver; accent deep royal blue, electric blue, subtle violet/blue gradients used carefully, avoid neon. Dark text with excellent contrast.

Layout: site content should occupy ~85% of viewport width on desktop (max-width 1500px, centered), 92% on smaller screens, spacious never edge-to-edge, responsive across desktop/laptop/tablet/mobile.

Typography: sleek professional fonts (Inter/Manrope/Plus Jakarta Sans for body, Space Grotesk for display), large bold display type, tight letter spacing, clean body text, strong hierarchy, minimal font weights, subtle gradient text on select important words.

Navigation: floating premium glassmorphic navbar with blur, thin border, rounded corners, sticky, shrinks and becomes more opaque on scroll. ROYAL300 logo left; links Services, Work, Clients, Pricing, Contact; right CTA "Let's Grow →".

Hero section: headline "WE DON'T JUST MARKET BRANDS. WE BUILD THEIR GROWTH." Supporting text: "ROYAL300 helps ambitious businesses build powerful digital identities, attract the right audience, generate meaningful leads, and turn online attention into measurable business growth." Primary CTA "Start Growing →", secondary CTA "Explore Our Work". Trust line: "Strategy • Creativity • Technology • Growth". Hero visual: an abstract 3D floating marketing ecosystem on the right with floating glass panels/cards showing metrics like +184% Reach, 3.2× Engagement, +67% Leads, 42K+ Campaign Impressions, floating 3D geometric objects, feeling like one cohesive sophisticated system with subtle floating animation.

Scroll experience: smooth scrolling, scroll-triggered animations, parallax, fade+translate, scale transitions, blur-to-sharp, staggered card reveals, sticky sections where appropriate. Slow, smooth, elegant, intentional easing (cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)), no bouncing.

Custom cursor: minimal futuristic pointer by default; expands over buttons; shows "VIEW" over project cards; becomes circular preview over images; subtle magnetic movement on links; small trailing effect. Disabled on touch devices.

Services section: heading "WE TURN DIGITAL PRESENCE INTO BUSINESS VALUE." Supporting text: "From brand positioning to performance campaigns, we combine creativity, technology and data to create digital systems that help businesses get noticed, trusted and chosen." Six premium service cards:
01 — Brand & Creative: "Build a brand people remember." Brand identity, creative direction, social media design, campaign creatives and visual systems designed to make businesses look credible and memorable.
02 — Digital Marketing: "Reach the people who matter." Strategic social media marketing, paid advertising and campaign management focused on awareness, engagement, leads and conversions.
03 — Website & Digital Experience: "Turn visitors into customers." High-performance websites and landing pages designed around user experience, trust and conversion.

06 — AI & Automation: "Work smarter. Scale faster." AI-powered workflows, automation, intelligent customer experiences and data-driven systems that reduce repetitive work and unlock new opportunities.

Value proposition section: heading "YOUR BUSINESS DESERVES MORE THAN JUST 'LIKES'." Show a before/after transformation. BEFORE: Low visibility, Inconsistent branding, Weak digital presence, Unqualified traffic, Manual processes. WITH ROYAL300: Strong brand positioning, Consistent digital identity, Targeted audience reach, Conversion-focused campaigns, Data-driven decisions, Automated workflows. Final statement: "We measure success by the value we create for your business — not simply by how good a campaign looks." Use animated numbers and connecting lines.

Our job is to transform that challenge into a digital experience that attracts attention, builds trust and drives action." Premium project grid, 4 columns desktop, 2 columns mobile, large image cards with image, project name, category, short description, key metric, hover interaction (image zoom, card lifts, gradient overlay, metric badges animate, cursor shows VIEW PROJECT, arrow rotates).

3D & motion: use subtle 3D effects throughout (CSS 3D transforms, perspective, Three.js/React Three Fiber where useful) — floating 3D marketing dashboard, rotating abstract sphere, glassmorphic floating cards, mouse-reactive elements, depth-based parallax, 3D project card tilt, floating particles, soft gradient blobs, perspective grids. Keep it subtle and premium, not gaming-style. Layered parallax across hero and project sections at different speeds, scroll-based transforms without jitter.

Micro-interactions: button hover text change (e.g. "Let's Grow →" becomes "Let's Grow ↗") with magnetic movement, card tilt/zoom/shadow/border-glow on hover, text reveal word-by-word / mask / slide-up animations, numbers counting from 0, image reveal clipping animations.

Scroll reveal system: sections fade/blur/scale/translate into view (blur 8px→0, opacity 0→1, y 60px→0, scale 0.96→1) with staggered delays for cards, never too fast.

Performance: lazy-load images, optimized image sizes/modern formats, minimal unnecessary JS, GPU-friendly transforms, Intersection Observer instead of expensive scroll listeners, respect prefers-reduced-motion, smooth 60fps, no layout shifts.

Responsive: desktop ~85% viewport width; project grid 4 columns desktop, 2-3 tablet, 2 mobile; hamburger nav on mobile; 3D elements scale down; hero becomes vertical; typography scales fluidly; generous spacing maintained; genuinely optimized mobile experience, not just a shrunk desktop layout.

Structure code cleanly into reusable components: Navbar, Hero, Services, ValueSection, Projects, Results, Clients, WhyRoyal300, Pricing, FinalCTA, Footer.

Design quality bar: this must not look like a generic Bootstrap/agency template, a SaaS dashboard, an overly colorful marketing site, or a template full of meaningless animations. It should feel like a premium creative technology agency site: clean, intelligent, confident, futuristic, trustworthy, high value. Every piece of copy should communicate business value (revenue, leads, visibility, customer acquisition, brand trust, conversion, efficiency, growth) rather than generic statements. The visitor journey should flow: attention (hero) → understanding (services) → value (value prop) → proof (projects/results) → trust (clients) → decision (pricing) → action (final CTA).
